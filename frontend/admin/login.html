Super Admin Security Protocol

Master Control Access: The Super Administrator account exercises root jurisdiction across all epidemiological subsystems:

  • Creation of authorized officer credentials and assignment of strict RBAC roles.
  • Unrestricted review of the tamper-evident cryptographic system audit log.
  • Dynamic outbreak area declarations and risk threshold parameterization.
  • Supervision of surveillance queues and quarantine referrals.
All administrative operations generate an immutable record with timestamps, user identities, and action signatures.
