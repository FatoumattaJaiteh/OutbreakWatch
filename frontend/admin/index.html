Super Administrator Architecture Guide

Root Administration Authority: As the Super Administrator, you are the highest privilege holder in the Ebola Traveller Screening & Surveillance ecosystem.

Key Governance Modules

  • Role Provisioning: Create accounts for officers across 4 functional tiers: Screening Officer, Surveillance Officer, Supervisor, and Auditor / DPO.
  • Audit Logging: Monitor an immutable ledger recording every login, triage event, risk override, and user creation event.
  • Surveillance Oversight: Inspect incoming declarations from Banjul International Airport (BJL) and track quarantine transfers.
  • Outbreak Area Triggers: Manage active country declarations (e.g. Uganda, DRC) that inform the 21-day timeline risk matrix.
