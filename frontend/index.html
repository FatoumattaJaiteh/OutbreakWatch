Public Health Guidelines

Essential Precautions & Prevention

Follow these approved health precautions to safeguard yourself, your loved ones, and the community during international travel.

Strict Hand Hygiene

Frequently wash your hands with clean water and soap, or use an alcohol-based hand sanitizer (at least 60% alcohol).

Avoid Bodily Fluids

Do not touch blood, vomit, sweat, saliva, or any bodily fluids of persons who appear unwell or suspected of infection.

Avoid Wild Animals

Do not handle fruit bats, monkeys, or apes in affected areas. Never handle or consume raw or inadequately cooked bushmeat.

Prompt Illness Reporting

If you develop a fever or feel sick within 21 days of travel, self-isolate immediately and call 1025 for confidential guidance.

Traveller Assistance

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about the screening system.

Who is required to fill out this declaration?

All arriving passengers disembarking at Banjul International Airport (BJL)—including Gambian citizens, residents, and visitors of all nationalities—are legally required to complete the health declaration for each journey.

When should I complete the form?

You can fill out the form up to 72 hours before your flight or on your smartphone upon arrival at the airport terminal. We recommend completing it prior to boarding for fastest clearance.

Does the QR code contain my personal medical information?

No. The QR pass contains an opaque, cryptographically salted token hash. It embeds zero readable personally identifiable information (PII). Only authorized health officers using authenticated Ministry terminals can look up your screening status.

What if I do not have a smartphone or data connection?

You can print your QR code confirmation page before departure. If you cannot access the form digitally, Port Health screening desks at Banjul Airport provide assisted entry desks with free terminal tablets.

24/7 Rapid Response Center

Need Public Health Assistance?

The National Public Health Emergency Operations Center (PHEOC) is on standby 24 hours a day to assist incoming travellers, airlines, and healthcare providers.
